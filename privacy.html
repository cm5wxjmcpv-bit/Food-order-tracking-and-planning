Community Meals Privacy

This program collects referring organization contact information and recipient delivery information to review free meal referrals, plan deliveries, and report program activity. Approved administrators can access saved records. Historical program records are retained unless the program changes its retention policy.

When enabled, address suggestions and address validation send the address text and an address-search session identifier to Google. Recipient names, phone numbers, dietary information, delivery instructions, and internal notes are not included in those requests. Do not put that information into an address field.

Only the final address you confirm is saved with the referral. Suggestion lists, Google response details, and Google coordinates are not saved in the spreadsheet. Verification receipts remain in memory in your browser. Retry protection stores a submission ID, a fingerprint, event information, and a receipt; it does not store recipient details in browser storage.

Google address services are subject to the Google Privacy Policy. There are no advertisements, recipient accounts, or recipient analytics in this application. Contact the community meal program administrator to request a correction or ask about access to program records.
