Community Meals Terms of Use

This application allows organizations to refer people for free community meals. A submitted referral reserves meals subject to administrator review; it does not guarantee approval or delivery.

Submit only the information necessary for delivery and information you are authorized to provide. Do not submit medical records or diagnoses. Review the final address, including any apartment or unit. Address verification does not guarantee deliverability.

When enabled, Google address features are governed by the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You may keep or edit an address when validation is uncertain. An administrator will review unresolved addresses.

Contact the community meal program administrator for assistance. See our Privacy notice for information about data handling.
